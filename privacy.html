IRONREP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Overview

IronRep is a workout tracking app developed by tmmr. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about data practices.

Data Collection

IronRep does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data is stored locally on your device only, including:

  • Your name (optional, for personalization only)
  • Training plans and exercises
  • Workout history including weights, reps, and sets
  • Settings (weight unit, theme, rest timer)
  • Custom exercise photos
  • Gym names and their approximate coordinates (see “Location” below)

Location

If you train at more than one gym, IronRep can tell them apart so that weights and records stay separated per location. This is optional: the app asks for permission only when you add a second gym, and everything keeps working without it — you then switch gyms manually.

  • Approximate location only. IronRep requests coarse location (Android: ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION; iOS: “When In Use”). It never requests precise, background or always-on location, and does no geofencing.
  • Only while you use the app. Your position is read when you finish a workout and when you start one — never in the background.
  • A gym’s coordinates are learned, not entered. Finishing a workout at a gym adds one sample to its running average. Manually added, imported, duplicated and shared workouts are excluded.
  • Nothing leaves your device. Coordinates are stored in the local database, are not transmitted anywhere, and are not linked to you or any identifier. They are included in a backup you create yourself, so a device change does not start over.
  • Removable at any time. Deleting a gym deletes its coordinates with it, and permission can be withdrawn in your device settings.

Advertising

IronRep displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can disable personalized ads in your device settings (iOS: "Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising"; Android: "Google > Ads"). Ads can be permanently removed through an in-app purchase.

Plan Sharing

When you share a training plan, exercise data (names, sets) is encoded into a link. This link does not contain any personal information.

Health Data (Apple Health & Health Connect)

IronRep can optionally integrate with Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). This integration is entirely opt-in. When enabled, IronRep may:

  • Write to Health: completed workouts (type, start and end time, duration, estimated calories) and body weight entries you record in IronRep.
  • Read from Health: body weight samples, so you don't have to enter them twice.

Health data stays on your device and is handled by the operating system's secure health store. IronRep does not transmit your health data to any external server. You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health settings (iOS: "Settings > Apple Health > Data Access & Devices > IronRep"; Android: Health Connect app). Health data is never shared with advertising, analytics or any third party.

In-App Purchases

IronRep offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob - Advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Apple / Google - In-App Purchases

Analytics

IronRep does not use any analytics tools such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar. No usage data is collected.

Data Deletion

All data is stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all data.

Children's Privacy

IronRep does not knowingly collect data from children. The ads shown are not personalized by default.
